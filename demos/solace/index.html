The Solace Approach

We don't treat symptoms in isolation. Every plan starts with a real conversation — and every practitioner works from the same page.

Step one — Understand

A real first conversation

A 45-minute assessment across movement, nutrition, and stress — so nothing gets treated in isolation.

Step two — Plan

One plan, whole picture

Your practitioners share one care plan and one file. Your physio knows what your nutritionist changed last week.

Step three — Progress

Measured, visible progress

Check-ins every four weeks with clear markers — pain scores, mobility ranges, sleep quality.
