About Nova

A small studio, built around one idea: the details matter.

Nova started from a simple frustration — most "custom" websites are really just templates with a new logo dropped in. We build differently. Every project starts as an actual design, built around your business, then coded by hand rather than assembled from a page builder.

We work with a small number of clients at a time, so every project gets real attention — from first sketch to the last pixel before launch.

How We Work

The principles behind every Nova project.

Design before code

We never start building until the design is approved. That means no surprises, and no compromises made mid-build.

Restraint over decoration

Every animation, every color, every component has a reason to exist. If it doesn't earn its place, it doesn't ship.

Fast is a feature

A beautiful site that loads slowly isn't a beautiful site. Performance is part of the design, not an afterthought.
